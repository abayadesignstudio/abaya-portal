The Firm

Good architecture should feel considered, lasting, and at home in its setting.

Abaya Design Studio is an architecture firm in Coppell, Texas, offering traditional design adapted for modern living. We draw from tradition not to repeat the past, but to recover what it teaches.

Whether residential, commercial, or institutional, our work seeks to serve real human life.
